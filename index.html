Meet the founder

Brian Brennan / Founder

Brian Brennan is a business strategist, analytics leader, and AI solutions developer with more than 20 years of experience helping organizations navigate complex challenges. His career spans marketing strategy, media operations, data analytics, and client-facing leadership, including building a digital media division and leading large-scale national accounts.

Today, Brian combines that experience with AI-assisted development to turn challenging business questions into practical, working solutions. From marketing accountability and startup go-to-market execution to interactive educational tools, his approach starts with understanding the problem—not forcing the technology.

He founded Solve·Logic around a simple belief: complex problems deserve intelligent, usable solutions.
